Overview

A residential villa development in northern Riyadh, positioned near Prince Faisal bin Bandar bin Abdulaziz Road and King Salman Road.

Concept & process

Contemporary villa façades draw on Salmani architectural character. Solid volumes, recessed openings and a restrained material language give the streets a consistent identity.

The masterplan places the individual villa types within a larger neighborhood framework. Typology diagrams compare the configurations and their relationship to the street.
