OverviewConcept & processPlansRenders

Overview

A mixed-use tower that reinterprets vernacular tectonic elements through contemporary architecture, responding to the Salmani Architecture Manifesto.

Concept & process

Natural textures become a system of order and rhythm. The façade’s varied vertical elements establish a distinct presence, while the program is organized into a clearly legible stack.

Program zoning connects the tower’s massing to its internal uses. The floor plans and companion sections trace the relationship between circulation, occupied space and the building envelope.
