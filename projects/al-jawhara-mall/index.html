Overview

A multi-use commercial center designed around contemporary daily life, cultural values and privacy.

Concept & process

A strong architectural mass is opened by sculptural voids. Spacious interiors and a gradual transition toward the center support circulation and interaction between the mall’s different functions.

Exterior, courtyard and interior perspectives examine how the building’s form frames shared spaces. The portfolio’s presentation focuses on spatial experience and architectural atmosphere.
