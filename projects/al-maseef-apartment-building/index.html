Overview

A residential apartment building on an 875 m² corner plot in Al Maseef, Riyadh. Ground and typical floors organize three apartments around shared circulation, while a setback roof level accommodates additional residential spaces and outdoor terraces.

Concept & process

The project combines compact apartment planning with a layered façade of light stone, recessed balconies, vertical screens and contrasting accents. The building addresses two street edges, with parking and entrances arranged around its corner setting.

The design develops the apartment layouts and shared stair and lift circulation alongside the roof arrangement. Floor plans and original BIM cutaways document the internal organization; sections trace the vertical relationships, and the supplied façade render set presents two finish treatments.

Challenges & achievements

Design challenge

Fit several independent apartments, shared services and parking onto a compact corner plot.

Design achievement

Three apartment layouts are arranged around shared stair and lift circulation, with parking organized along the street edges.

Design challenge

Combine household privacy with daylight and outdoor space.

Design achievement

Recessed balconies, façade screens and a setback roof level provide outdoor connections while maintaining separation between apartments.
