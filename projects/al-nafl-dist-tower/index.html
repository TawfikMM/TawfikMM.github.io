Overview

A mixed-use proposal in Al Nafl District, Riyadh, combining retail, offices and apartments on a 4,400 m² site. A shared podium supports two upper building volumes, with three basement parking levels, a mechanical floor and roof services.

Concept & process

Stepped masses and a central opening create a vertical composition above the podium. Deep horizontal planted terraces and vertical screens shape the façades, while the ground-level entrance and outdoor spaces connect the development to the street.

The form-development sequence tests hierarchy, shading, outdoor areas and connections between the volumes. Site zoning separates the mixed-use programme and landscape areas; the floor plans document changes in use across the podium and upper floors, supported by sections and elevations.
