OverviewConcept & processPlansRenders

Overview

A center conceived as a space for healing, unity and personal growth in an area shaped by historical conflict.

Concept & process

Four themes—physical, mental, social and individual growth—are expressed through distinct architectural forms. A central sphere and rising ramp symbolize the individual journey toward holistic growth.

The plan organizes the center around its shared core. Exterior and interior views explore the relationship between grounded volumes, floating forms and the spherical exhibition space.

Awarded TOP TEN GRADUATION PROJECTS IN THE FACULTY OF ARCHITECTURE at Eastern Mediterranean University (EMU).

Level

Design studies

The site in Varosha, Famagusta, grounds the project in a history of conflict. Physical, mental, social and individual development organize the design: grounded volumes express resilience, while open spaces support freedom and community. The central sphere and ramped exhibition sequence represent personal growth and a shared future.
