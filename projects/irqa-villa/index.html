Overview

A private villa in the Irqa District, approximately 3.5 kilometers from the Western Ring Road.

Concept & process

The external façades blend traditional Saudi aesthetics with a contemporary architectural approach. Salmani references establish a sense of cultural continuity in the home’s proportions and details.

The BIM studies unfold the building level by level. Axonometric views, plans and a dimensioned elevation connect the exterior character to the villa’s internal organization.
