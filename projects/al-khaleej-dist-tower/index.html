Overview

A mixed-use development in Al Khaleej District, Riyadh, bringing street-level retail and upper-level residences together on a 4,000 m² site. The proposal combines two basement parking levels, a dedicated mechanical floor and a roof level within a unified building envelope.

Concept & process

A sand-toned façade, horizontal bands and geometric screens reinterpret local architectural motifs. Single- and double-height planted recesses break the long elevation into a measured rhythm, adding depth and shaded outdoor connections.

Site access, sun orientation and the relationship to the surrounding streets inform the massing. The presentation develops the form from a simple stacked envelope into recessed openings, planted spaces and an articulated façade, then documents the proposal through floor plans, sections and elevations.
