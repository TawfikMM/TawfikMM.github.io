Overview

A residential development within a large suburban community in northeastern Riyadh, bringing contemporary living together with the cultural character of Salmani architecture.

Concept & process

Green spaces and pedestrian connections form a shared landscape for the residential community. Housing is conceived as part of an integrated setting for daily life.

Villa-type studies pair architectural plans with exterior views, showing how different configurations contribute to the wider residential fabric.
